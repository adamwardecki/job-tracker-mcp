You set the direction.

Choose where to look, what makes a good match, and how you want to apply. Once set up, Job Tracker follows that plan on a schedule and keeps track of what happens next.

  1. Your search scheduleWeekdays at 07:00
    MTWTFSS
    Job boards + company careers pages
    01

    Looks where you ask.

    At the times you choose, Job Tracker checks the job boards and company careers pages you selected during setup. You don’t need to start a new search each day.

  2. Your filters, every time✓ Marketing & operations✓ Remote in Europe✓ Meets your salary rangeChecked against the employer’s posting
    02

    Finds the roles that fit.

    Each role is checked against your setup filters, including role type, location, salary, and deal-breakers. Job Tracker verifies the employer’s posting and skips roles you’re already pursuing.

  3. Your application preferences
    ✓ Answers filled in from your profile
    ✓ Application checked
    Review firstorApply automatically
    03

    Fills the forms. You set the rules.

    For matching roles, Job Tracker fills out application forms using your CV and saved answers. Choose to review each application before it’s sent, or enable automatic submission during setup. Anything it can’t complete comes back to you.

  4. Every role has a next step
    Matched↓Applied↓Interview
    04

    Follows what happens next.

    Job Tracker logs each role from match to application, interview, and outcome. With your inbox and calendar connected, it checks for replies and interview updates on each run, updates your tracker, and flags what needs your attention.

The tools you already use.

Connect your calendar, email, and browser, and let Job Tracker do more of the legwork. With your connectors set up, it can follow replies, keep track of interviews, and help with applications in one place.

  • Calendar

    Keep upcoming interviews in view, so you know what’s next and have time to prepare.

  • Email

    Catch recruiter replies, update your application progress, and draft responses for you to review and send.

  • Browser

    Reach job boards you’re signed into, check the employer’s posting, and fill in application forms with your saved details.

A calmer search ahead.

Works with Claude Desktop’s Code tab, Claude Code, and Hermes Agent. Choose your setup below.

1

Open Claude’s Code tab

Open the Claude desktop app ↗ and sign in. Choose Code, select Local, then choose a folder for your job search. You can create one called “My job search”.

ChatCoworkCode⌂ Local

You’ll use the Code tab, but you won’t need to write code.

2

Add Job Tracker

Next to the message box, open + → Plugins → Add plugin. Add the Job Tracker repository as a marketplace, then select job-tracker and install it for your user account.

https://github.com/adamwardecki/job-tracker-mcp

A marketplace is simply where Claude finds the add-on.

3

Let Job Tracker get to know you

Start a fresh local session. Copy this into Claude’s message box and press Enter. It will guide you through adding your CV, choosing your preferences, and trying a small first search.

/job-tracker:setup

If it asks for Node.js, follow the guided installer, then quit and reopen Claude to continue.

4

Choose when to look

Choose your sources, filters, and a local search schedule. Decide whether to review applications before submission or enable automatic applications for matching roles. You can also start a search yourself.

For your morning shortlist

Scheduled searches run on this computer. Keep Claude Desktop open and your computer awake at the time you choose.

Claude’s guide to desktop plugins ↗
1

Open Claude Code

Open a terminal in your job-search folder and start Claude Code with claude. Requires Claude Code 2.1.295 or newer.

2

Install Job Tracker

Run these commands one at a time inside Claude Code.

/plugin marketplace add adamwardecki/job-tracker-mcp
/plugin install job-tracker@job-tracker-mcp
3

Start your guided setup

/job-tracker:setup

Bring your CV and choose your preferences. Setup checks Node.js and helps with installation on macOS and Windows if needed.

Good to know

Bring your CV.
We’ll start there.

You can pause setup and come back whenever you like.

What does it cost?
Job Tracker is free. Claude requires a plan with Code access; your assistant’s usage limits or provider charges still apply.
Will it send applications?
You choose during setup: review each application before submission, or enable automatic applications for matching roles. Emails stay as drafts for you to send.
Where is my information?
Your tracker is saved on your computer. Information used in a conversation is also processed by your AI provider.
