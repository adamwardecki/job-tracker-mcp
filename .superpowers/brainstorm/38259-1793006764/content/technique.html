Particles or shader? Both running on your illustration

Quick prototypes in plain three.js (the real one will be R3F). Laptop screen is kept crisp in both. Watch for 10 seconds, then click the one you prefer, or tell me in the terminal.

A · Brush-stroke particles

Instanced strokes in three passes (broad underpainting → medium → fine detail where the image is busy). Each stroke takes its colour from the illustration with slight broken-colour jitter and follows the contours. Strokes breathe in the wind, more in the bamboo.

B · Painterly shader

One full-screen fragment shader: Kuwahara filter (flattens areas into paint patches), stroke texture aligned to the image gradient, canvas grain, and a slow noise warp so it shimmers.
